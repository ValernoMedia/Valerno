Questions

Fair questions.

I already use Booksy or Square. Do I have to switch?

No. We build around what you already use and make it way easier for clients to find, from your site to your Instagram to Google.

We already take reservations. Does that change?

Not unless you want it to. If you already use OpenTable, Resy or Toast, we connect it properly so it's front and center everywhere people look.

What if I don't need booking?

Then we'll make it dead simple for people to call, message or request a quote instead. The goal is the same: turn visitors into customers.

Do I own the website?

Yes. It's your site and your domain. If you ever stop working with us, it stays yours.

How long does it take?

About a week from our first call. Most of that depends on how fast we get your photos and info.

I already have a website. Is this still for me?

Maybe. We'll tell you honestly whether it's worth rebuilding. Sometimes it just needs booking added and a cleanup.

Are there monthly fees?

The build is a one-time price. Monthly marketing is optional, month-to-month, and you can cancel anytime.
